Personal injury

When someone else’s negligence changes your life, clear guidance can help you focus on recovery instead of the insurance process.

On this page

  • Overview
  • Automobile accidents
  • The insurance process
  • General negligence

Mr. D’Angelo handles personal injury claims involving automobile accidents, general negligence, and professional negligence. He has worked with major insurance carriers and widely used regional carriers throughout Northern California.

His approach begins with listening carefully to what happened, how the injury has affected you, and what the insurance company is asking you to do. From there, he explains the claim process and helps you understand each decision.

Automobile accidents

For more than 20 years, Mr. D’Angelo has represented clients injured in single-vehicle and multivehicle accidents. If you have been injured in an automobile or motorcycle accident, he can help you work through both the property-damage and bodily-injury portions of the claim.

The goal is to make a difficult process understandable and manageable—from the first insurance conversation through the final resolution.

Understanding the insurance process

Accident claims can involve several different kinds of coverage. Mr. D’Angelo helps clients understand property damage coverage, medical payments coverage, uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage, and bodily injury coverage.

You do not have to sort through every insurance question alone.

A free consultation gives you a chance to explain the situation and understand whether legal representation may help.

When medical providers are involved, he also works through the financial details of the claim with the aim of protecting the client’s recovery.

General negligence

Some injuries happen because another person or organization failed to exercise reasonable care. These matters may include slips and falls, intentional or negligent infliction of emotional distress, or professional negligence.

Since 2002, Mr. D’Angelo has helped clients navigate these claims with careful attention to the facts, compassion for the person affected, and straightforward communication from the initial consultation through resolution.
