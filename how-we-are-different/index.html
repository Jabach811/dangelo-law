Our approach

Legal counsel should feel personal, understandable, and available when you need it most.

A more personal practice

Service built around the person, not the process.

Mr. D’Angelo takes the role of both attorney and counselor seriously. That means listening before advising and speaking in plain language instead of hiding behind legal terminology.

In-home visits

Recent injuries, transportation challenges, age, or disability can make a trip to the office difficult. Mr. D’Angelo offers in-home consultations for personal injury and estate planning matters when a house call makes better sense.

The goal is simple: access to legal guidance should not depend on whether getting across town is easy.

Plain-language counsel

Legal matters come with complicated rules and unfamiliar terms. Vincent’s job is to explain what they mean for you, what choices are available, and what happens next.

You should be able to leave a conversation with a clearer understanding—not a longer list of words to look up.

Communication that stays personal

Since opening the practice in 2002, Mr. D’Angelo has treated communication as the foundation of the attorney-client relationship. Questions are answered directly, and clients are kept informed as their matter moves forward.

That responsiveness is especially important in personal injury and estate matters, where delays and uncertainty can add to an already difficult moment.

Evening and weekend appointments

Many Tracy-area residents commute long distances and cannot easily step away during a standard workday. Evening and weekend appointments may be available when scheduling during office hours is difficult.

Call the office to discuss a time that works for your situation.
